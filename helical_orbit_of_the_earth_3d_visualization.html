Controls & Concepts

How to Use This Visualization

Camera Navigation

Left-drag / 1-finger drag: Orbit around the solar system.  •  Scroll / Pinch: Zoom in and out.  •  Right-drag / Shift-drag: Pan the view.

Reference Frame Presets

  • Galactic (83.2°): True scale of the Sun orbiting the Milky Way center at ~251 km/s (52.95 AU/yr).
  • Local Stars / LSR (33.2°): The Sun’s peculiar motion relative to nearby stars (~19.5 km/s, 4.11 AU/yr).
  • Tight Coil (10.0°): Compresses the forward axis so Earth’s spiral turns are tightly stacked for geometric inspection.

Sliders & Playback

  • Sun Speed / Axial Scale: Adjusts the Sun’s forward speed (and thus the axial stretch/compression along its path). 1.00× represents true Galactic proportions.
  • Time Span: Sets the total duration rendered along the trajectory from 1 to 100 Earth years.
  • Pause / Play & Reset View: Freezes or resumes motion along the path, or reframes the camera to fit the current time span.

Bodies & Labels

  • ☽ Moon: Toggles Earth’s Moon (27.32-day period, ~13.37 loops per Earth year; orbital radius slightly expanded for visibility).
  • Inner: Toggles Mercury, Venus, and Mars at their true orbital radii and speeds.
  • Outer: Toggles Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune at their true orbital radii and speeds.
  • Symbols / Names: Independently toggle astronomical glyphs (☉ ⊕ ☽ ☿ ♀ ♂ ♃ ♄ ♅ ♆) and full body names.
